Scadable is building the compliance engine for companies that ship connected products. Not another scanner that hands you a list of problems, the engine that does the compliance work itself.

The idea is simple. Compliance does not live in a dashboard. It lives in the legal, privacy, and security requests that land in your inboxes, from regulators, customers, auditors, and your own team. Scadable connects to those channels, handles the work autonomously, and pulls a human in only when someone genuinely needs to be involved. When a question comes in that the system can answer, it answers. When something needs a decision, it escalates. That is the direction: your compliance function, run as software.

## Starting with the Cyber Resilience Act

Every engine needs a first job. Ours is the [EU Cyber Resilience Act](https://scadable.com/frameworks/cyber-resilience-act) (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), the new EU law that makes cybersecurity a legal requirement for any product with digital elements sold in the EU.

Scadable closes the loop the CRA demands, end to end:

- **Maps the fleet.** Every device, deployment, and component you have shipped, in one live graph, so you can see exactly what is exposed.
- **Catches what is actively exploited.** Not every CVE, the ones being exploited right now. That is the signal that matters, and it is the CRA reporting trigger.
- **Fixes it.** Writes the fix, backports it to fielded devices (including old, end-of-life ones with no upstream patch), and opens a pull request you approve.
- **Files the report.** Generates the report and evidence automatically, inside the 24-hour window the CRA requires for actively exploited vulnerabilities.
- **Gets you certified.** When you are ready, Scadable lines up the conformity assessment with a vetted partner.

The first hard CRA deadline, the 24-hour reporting obligation, applies from **11 September 2026**. The full essential requirements apply from **11 December 2027**.

## Why the CRA first

Because it is the right beachhead. The CRA is highly automatable, it is new enough that no one owns the space yet, and it rewards moving fast, the reporting clock is the pain that arrives first and the one most teams are least ready for. It is a real, urgent problem with a clean automation surface, which is exactly where an engine like this proves itself before expanding across the rest of a company's compliance load.
