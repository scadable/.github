# Scadable's vision: the intelligence layer for companies

Scadable is a search company. Compliance is its first application.

## What is the intelligence layer?

The intelligence layer is an index of how a company actually works: its systems, documents and decisions. It answers questions about the company with the source attached. Every answer is correct, complete and cited, or the system says it does not know. Scadable is building it.

## Why is this a search problem?

A company already holds the answers to most of the questions it asks about itself. Who can reach production, which change caused an outage, what was promised to a customer, whether a control ran last quarter. The hard part is finding the exact source, showing it, and being honest when nothing answers. That is search, held to a higher standard than a list of links.

## Why start with compliance?

Compliance is the strictest reader a search system can have. An auditor checks every claim, asks where each answer came from, and rejects anything approximate. Answers that hold up in an audit hold up anywhere. Compliance is also work companies already need done, so the intelligence layer is useful from the first day instead of after years of research.

## What does Scadable do today?

Scadable is the compliance engine that fixes what it finds. It identifies what does not meet a standard across a company's code, cloud and identity systems, fixes it, reports with evidence, and prepares the company for an independent audit firm to examine. The first framework is SOC 2, the report enterprise buyers ask for before they sign.

## What is the standard Scadable holds itself to?

1. **Cited.** Every answer points to the source it came from.
2. **Complete or explicitly incomplete.** A count or a list is exact, or the answer says what it could not see.
3. **Abstain rather than guess.** When the sources do not support an answer, the system says so.
4. **Accountable.** A person can check any answer against its source, and the record of what was answered does not change afterwards.

## Where is Scadable going?

The questions an auditor asks are a strict subset of the questions a company asks every day. The same layer that answers them for compliance answers them for the people and the agents working in the company. Compliance is where the layer earns trust. The intelligence layer is the company.
